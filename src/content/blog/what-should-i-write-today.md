> In the world without gold, we might have been heroes ~ Edward Thatch

In today's world, generative artificial intelligence contains a solution of almost every problem. One who wouldn't spend thousand of hours learning how to draw can make a stunning image of a pigeon. Artist can make a motion graphic just using a single line of incomplete sentence. Programmer no longer need hours to write a bunch of codes. Yet, a question remains in my mind, if this machine can replace almost all of human's jobs, how human can grow it's problem solving ability?

# Problems

Writing report already became hideous thing on my work. I never really like it to begin with. So, I grab the documents and tell the so-called Generative Artificial Intelligence to write report for me. After a few minutes, I check on the result get stunned with the result which normally could take me for a few weeks to finished it. Even the ppt looks cooler that what I did in one day straight. 
I love reading books, novels, mangas, etc. But, research article is different. It so heavy I barely understand a few words within an hour. So, ONCE AGAIN, I tell AI to read for me and give the tldr. Amazing, I don't have to read those things anymore. I can continue playing or doing some stupid things. 
The cycle repeats in every part of life that I have. There is a time, or should I say **now**, when I realize this is bad for my development. Things should not be this easy for me because the detrimental effect of AI over usage come when I'm not using it anymore. Every task becomes heavy. Every idea seems to feel like it sucks. What I have done to myself? I wonder. Should I use Generative Artificial Intelligence to improve my writing? my reading? my everyday task? my very own decision? There is something I need to change. I just don't know what or how.

# Offered Solution

Simple. Just write. Every AI generated text is created with a lot of calculation. Human brain does not work like that. It can create a bliss of pure ignorance. A piece of human stupidity that is still untouch by artificial mind. This is my starting point of a journey to improve a writing of mine. Yet, to improve means to know that I can do better. Current LLMs are excel in that field. Spotting some kind of miscalculated words, grammar errors, awkward collocations, etc is one of task that I can't do alone. 

I really hope this works.
